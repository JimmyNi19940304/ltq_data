Peak-finding settings

Scans after the retention-time limit are dropped before anything else happens, so nothing past it reaches a chromatogram, a peak or a spectrum. Set it to 0 to keep the whole run. Each compound peak's spectrum window starts this many widths at half maximum wide, centred on the peak's top; it can be dragged wider or narrower on the chromatogram afterwards.
