Peak-finding settings

Scans after the retention-time limit are dropped before anything else happens, so nothing past it reaches a chromatogram, a peak or a spectrum. Set it to 0 to keep the whole run. Peak window height sets where the accumulation window is cut: 0.9 takes nearly the whole peak, 0.5 would be the width at half maximum.
